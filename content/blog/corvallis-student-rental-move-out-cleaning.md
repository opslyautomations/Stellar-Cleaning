Corvallis move out cleaning is busiest in June, when Oregon State University's spring term ends and many off-campus leases wrap up within a few weeks of each other. If you rent near campus, plan the clean as carefully as the move itself: decide who cleans what, know what your landlord will inspect, and book any outside help well before finals week. Here is how to handle it.

## Why June is the crunch for Corvallis move out cleaning

OSU runs on a quarter system, and spring term finals fall in June. You can confirm the exact dates for your year on the [OSU academic calendar](https://registrar.oregonstate.edu/osu-academic-calendar). Many apartments and houses around campus lease from late summer through June or July to match the school year, so a large share of student renters move out in the same short window.

That timing creates three practical problems:

- **Everyone needs the same days.** Moving trucks, storage units and cleaning crews fill up for the weekend after finals.
- **Your time is short.** You are packing during finals week, and the clean usually has to happen after the furniture is out.
- **Roommates leave at different times.** One person flies home the day after their last exam, another stays for a summer job, and the clean falls on whoever is left.

The fix is to treat the move-out clean as a scheduled task with a date, not something you squeeze in on the last afternoon.

## How roommates can split the cleaning fairly

In most shared rentals, every roommate signed the same lease and the landlord holds one deposit. That means a dirty bathroom down the hall can cost everyone, not just the person who used it. Settle the plan before finals.

1. **Divide by room, not by time.** Each person takes their own bedroom plus one shared area: kitchen, bathroom, living room or entry and laundry.
2. **Give the kitchen to two people.** The oven, refrigerator, cabinets and floor are the biggest job in any rental.
3. **Agree on a finish date.** Everyone's assigned rooms should be done before the first person leaves town.
4. **Do a walk-through together.** Check each other's rooms against the same checklist so nobody is surprised at inspection.
5. **Photograph the finished unit.** Dated photos of every room protect everyone if a deduction is disputed later.
6. **Decide now how a deduction gets split.** A quick written agreement in a group chat saves a lot of arguments in July.

If the schedule makes that impossible, many roommate groups simply split the cost of a professional clean instead. It removes the question of who did their share.

## What Corvallis landlords inspect at move-out

Landlords and property managers work from the condition the unit was in when you moved in, so pull out your move-in condition report if you have one. Expect them to look closely at:

- The oven interior, stovetop and range hood
- The refrigerator and freezer, including drawers and door seals
- Inside kitchen and bathroom cabinets and drawers
- Tubs, showers, grout, toilets and sinks
- Baseboards, window sills and window tracks
- Carpets and hard floors, including inside closets
- Walls for holes, marks and anything taped or stuck on
- Trash, food and belongings left behind, inside and outside

Under Oregon law, a landlord can deduct from the deposit only what is reasonably necessary to remedy lease defaults and repair damage beyond ordinary wear and tear, and must send the deposit or a written accounting within 31 days. Carpet cleaning deductions come with extra conditions. Those rules are in [ORS 90.300](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors090.html), and our [Oregon move out cleaning checklist](/blog/move-out-cleaning-checklist-oregon) summarizes them in plain language. This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current statute or talk to a professional if you are disputing a charge.

## Local help if a deposit dispute comes up

The City of Corvallis runs a [Rental Housing Program](https://www.corvallisoregon.gov/node/10071) that answers basic rental questions and points tenants and landlords to resources on their rights and responsibilities. It is a good first stop if you are unsure whether a deduction is fair. OSU students can also check what support their student government and the university offer for off-campus renters.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I book Corvallis move out cleaning for June?

Book Corvallis move out cleaning as soon as you know your move-out date, ideally in spring and well before OSU finals week. The days just after spring term ends are the most requested because so many leases end together. Booking early gives you a better choice of dates and leaves time for a walkthrough and a firm quote.

### Do all roommates have to pay if one person leaves the unit dirty?

Often yes, because roommates on the same lease usually share one security deposit and the landlord deducts from it as a whole. How you split a deduction among yourselves is up to your group. Agree in writing before move-out, and have everyone finish their rooms before the first person leaves Corvallis.

### Can I clean my rental before my roommates move out?

You can clean your own bedroom and your share of the common areas early, but the final clean works best once everyone's belongings are out. Shared spaces like the kitchen and bathroom get used until the last person leaves. Schedule a final pass for that last day so the unit is inspection-ready when keys are returned.

### What should I leave behind for the landlord at move-out?

Leave the keys, garage remotes and anything that came with the unit, plus a written forwarding address for your deposit or accounting. Remove all of your belongings, food and trash, including from outdoor areas. Leave nothing taped to walls. Take dated photos of each clean room before you hand back the keys.

### Does a professional move-out clean include carpet shampooing?

A standard professional move-out clean includes thorough vacuuming and spot treatment, but not machine shampooing or hot-water extraction. If your Corvallis lease requires carpet cleaning by machine, book a carpet-cleaning specialist for that part. Check the lease wording first, since Oregon law sets specific conditions on carpet cleaning deductions.
